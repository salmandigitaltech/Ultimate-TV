## 📱 How to Install the Package

### 1. On Android Smartphones & Tablets
1. Download the `UltimateTV.apk` file from the link above.
2. Tap on the downloaded `.apk` file to open it.
3. If prompted, enable **"Allow installation from unknown sources"** in your device settings.
4. Tap **Install** and open **Ultimate TV** to start streaming.

### 2. On Android TV / Smart TV / TV Box / Firestick
1. Download `UltimateTV.apk` on your computer or phone and copy it to a **USB Drive**.
2. Plug the USB drive into your Android TV or TV Box.
3. Open any **File Manager** app on your TV (e.g., *File Commander* or *FX File Explorer*).
4. Locate `UltimateTV.apk` on your USB drive and select it to install.
5. Alternatively, use apps like **Send Files to TV** to transfer the package wirelessly from your smartphone to your TV.
